DM Tools

Prepare and mix the soundscape of your tabletop RPG scenes.

DM Tools isn't a turnkey app: it's a tool for building the audio of your sessions yourself, scene by scene.

Download Explore the features

Features

Today, DM Tools focuses on one specific need: preparing the audio for your TTRPG scenes.

🎚️

Audio mixer

Layer multiple soundscapes live — tavern, forest, combat — to set the mood instantly.

📼

Track library

Centralize the audio tracks for your scenes, reusable across campaigns.

🗂️

Campaign organization

Group your audio scenes by campaign so you can find them quickly mid-session.

🔒

100% offline

No data ever sent anywhere: everything stays stored locally, on your device.
